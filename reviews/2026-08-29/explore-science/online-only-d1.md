# D1 — Tonal Register of Informal Phrasing in Technical Sections

Source: authenticated Explore Science project review, accessed 2026-08-29.
This issue is counted in the report's 11-issue total but omitted from the
downloadable PDF's 10 detailed comments.

Severity: Minor

Category: Reporting Quality / Discussion

Locations: pages 6 and 21 of the reviewed manuscript.

## Critique

Two colloquial constructions in the discussion prose could be replaced with
more formally precise language to better match the register of the surrounding
formal treatment.

## Justification

The review identifies “Church--Rosser-shaped” in Section 3 and “cannot be
wished away” in Section 9.5 as contextually clear but less formal than the rest
of the manuscript.

## Recommended actions

1. Replace “Church--Rosser-shaped” with language stating that the proposition is
   structurally analogous to the Church--Rosser property.
2. Replace “cannot be wished away” with language stating that ordering and
   effect contracts remain non-eliminable operational obligations.
